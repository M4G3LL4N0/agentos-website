The loop is the product.

        

          Not a chat. Not a standing crew of researcher / builder / red-team.
          Those roles can appear on a plan. They are not the architecture.
        

        
    
      
    

      
    
        
    SIMULATED is the default.
    
        
    
          LIVE needs explicit authorization. Detection of a binary is never
          READY. Cost is UNKNOWN unless a charge was measured. The echo
          session above completed an objective and still recorded
          result_state=PARTIAL because the mode was simulated.
        
    
        
      
      
      

      
      
        
      Three surfaces. One core.
      
        
      
          CLI, HTTP, and MCP are thin. Business logic lives in
          services.AgentOS. A new interface that bypasses that
          facade is a defect.
        
      
        
        
      
        

      
        
        
        Readiness is measured, then claimed.
        
        
        
          From agentos doctor on 2026-09-25 in an isolated home.
          Health is what a probe returned. Readiness is what AgentOS may say.
          xai is AUTH_REQUIRED. langgraph is
          UNAVAILABLE. Those are not failures of the page.
